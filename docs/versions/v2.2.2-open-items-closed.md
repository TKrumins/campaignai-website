## What changed

### 1. The America 250 clock now rolls over instead of stopping

**The problem:** the countdown was tied to Election Day and took the offer down
with it on November 3. The Special now runs six weeks past that, to December 31.

**The fix:** the clock has four stages instead of one on/off switch.

| Stage | When | What people see |
|---|---|---|
| Counting to Election Day | until Nov 3 | "Election Day is coming" + the clock |
| Election Day | Nov 3 | "It's Election Day." The clock steps aside — the day is a moment, not a countdown |
| Counting the Special down | Nov 4 – Dec 31 | "America 250 ends December 31" + days remaining |
| Over | after Dec 31 | The announcement bar folds itself away |

Election Day stays the headline moment, which was the whole reason for keeping
it. What changed is that the clock keeps running afterwards against the
Special's own deadline.

When the Special ends, the announcement bar collapses using the same mechanism
as when a visitor dismisses it, so it does not leave an empty strip across every
page.

### 2. The pricing cards now say how many revisions you get

"Two editorial revisions" on Professional Video, "One editorial revision" on
Candidate Campaigns. The nonprofit card stays silent — its pricing is case by
case, so its revision count is too.

### 3. The delivery line no longer mentions a checkout

It offered to arrange weekend turnaround "at checkout." There is no checkout.
It now points at the onboarding call, which is where every path leads.

### 4. The copy reference document is current again

`docs/handoff/PAGE-COPY.md` carries the September corrections in its live
sections. The retired `/purchase` section and the frozen `/for/*` sections were
deliberately left alone — they record what was built — and the header now says
so plainly, so nobody quotes those prices as current. Its `PURCHASE_URL` was
also still pointing at the retired `/purchase` route.

### 5. The homepage stat — with a correction

Tom approved replacing the weak "1" with **"100% of creative decisions made by a
human being."** That wording is now in the file.

**But the correction matters more than the change.** That stat band is not on
the website. `StatsMomentSection` is not imported anywhere — it renders on no
page. My 2.2.1 note said it was on the home page. That was wrong.

The change was still made, so the file is not carrying a stale promise if it is
ever brought back. But the line is not live, and **it still needs a home.**
