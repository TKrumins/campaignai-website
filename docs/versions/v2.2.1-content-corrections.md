# Version 2.2.1 — Content corrections

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Branch:** `content/v2.2.1-corrections`
**Status:** built and verified, not yet live

A correction pass on what the website promises. No new pages, no new sections,
nothing moved. Five facts changed, everywhere they appeared.

---

## What changed

### 1. Turnaround is 72 hours, not 48

Every promise about how fast a finished video comes back moved from 48 hours to
72. That is 23 separate places, including the line shared across several pages,
the description search engines show, the homepage counter, and the "every video
includes" list.

### 2. The onboarding call is 60 minutes, not 30

Nine places. The brief asked to change "20 minutes" to 60, but no 20-minute
copy was left on the site — an earlier pass had already moved it to 30. So 30 is
what actually changed.

### 3. Candidate campaigns are $999 per video, not $599

The crossed-out $1,999 comparison price stays everywhere it appeared. Three
numbers that are *calculated* from the rate were recalculated rather than
hand-edited:

- "You save $1,400" became **"You save $1,000"**
- "70% off the standard rate" became **"50% off"** (two places)
- The Story Arc Builder's per-chapter running total now uses the new rate

### 4. America 250 is open to everyone, through the end of the year

The offer itself is unchanged — buy two videos, the first is $250. What changed
is who can take it and for how long. It is no longer capped at the first 250
buyers; it now runs through December 31, 2026, which is the real hook. Slate
purchases and party-affiliated purchases are excluded, and that is stated on all
four places the offer appears.

### 5. One editorial revision, two for organizations

The old promise was "3 revisions in production + 1 in post." It is now one
editorial revision for candidate campaigns, and two for organizations buying at
the full rate. It replaces the old promise outright rather than just the
post-production half.

Six of the seven places this appears talk to candidates and organizations on
the same screen, so each one states both counts in a single line rather than
guessing who is reading. The revision is written as part of what you get, never
as a cap.

### 6. New: when pricing is effective and how long it holds

"Pricing effective September 2026. Pricing holds through December 31, 2026."

This is new copy, not a replacement — the brief expected to find similar wording
already on the site and there was none. It appears on the pricing page and the
get-started page only: the two places someone is actually deciding what to pay.

## Where to look

The pricing page, the get-started page, the home page, and how-it-works carry
almost all of it. The compliance page and the video production process page each
changed by one line.

---

## Deliberately left alone

- **The Election Day countdown.** It marks the election, not the offer. Tom's
  call: the election date matters more than tidiness here.
- **The "48 Hours Left" example ad headline** on the product section. That is
  fundraising urgency copy in a sample ad, not a delivery promise.
- **"15-, 30-, and 60-second cuts."** Video lengths, not call lengths.
- **Nonprofit pricing.** No number appears anywhere on the site, in any config
  file, or in any structured data. The nonprofit card still reads "Let's talk,"
  priced case by case. Nothing needed removing — it was already clean.
- **The build-instruction documents** in `docs/build-instructions/`. Those are a
  historical record of what was asked for at the time and are not meant to track
  the live site.
- **Existing "this cycle" and "2026 cycle" phrasing.** Left in place alongside
  the new validity line.

## Worth knowing

- **There is no structured pricing data on this site.** No schema.org markup, no
  pricing config file, no checkout system. The brief asked about all three.
  Prices live in components and in one shared constants file.
- **Hidden pages were updated too.** The Story Arc Builder is currently dark —
  unlinked and hidden from search — but its price calculation was corrected so it
  is not wrong if it is ever switched back on.

## Still open

These were flagged rather than guessed. None of them block the pass.

### 1. The homepage counter now counts up to "1"

The homepage stat band shows four numbers: 72hr, $999, 50 states, and — now — 1
editorial revision. A "1" reads weakly next to the other three, and it is the
only one that sounds like a limit rather than a strength.

**Suggested fix:** replace that stat entirely. "100% human-finished" or "0
watermarks" would carry the same reassurance without counting down. Not done,
because swapping a homepage stat is a content decision, not a correction.

### 2. America 250 will disappear on November 3

The offer box hides itself automatically the moment the Election Day clock hits
zero. The offer now runs through December 31, so as written it would vanish six
weeks early.

**This needs a decision before November 3, 2026.** Left untouched per Tom's
instruction to preserve the Election Day clock.

### 3. The delivery line still mentions a checkout that does not exist

The shared delivery sentence ends with "Need a weekend turnaround? We can
arrange it at checkout." There is no checkout — invoicing happens after the
onboarding call. Out of scope for a numbers pass, but it is wrong.

### 4. The pricing cards never state a revision count

The candidate card and the professional card show price, savings and what the
rate covers, but say nothing about revisions. Now that the two tiers get
different amounts, those cards are the natural place to say so. Adding it would
be new copy, so it was not done.

### 5. The copy reference document is now out of date

`docs/handoff/PAGE-COPY.md` records the website's copy as of July. It still says
48 hours, $599, and 3+1 revisions throughout. It should be regenerated or
retired.

## How this was verified

The site was built twice — once from `main`, once from this branch — and the two
sets of generated pages were compared directly.

**39 pages compared. Layout and styling are identical on every one**, apart from
two paragraph elements intentionally added to carry the pricing validity line.

Also confirmed in the finished build:

- No "$599", "48 hours", "3 revisions", "30-minute", or "first 250 customers"
  anywhere.
- The validity line appears exactly once on pricing and once on get-started, and
  nowhere else.
- Type checking passes. Five pre-existing code warnings are unchanged — all of
  them are in files this pass never touched.
