6.4Option 1 reworked · Through the Voter's Eyes

Your instinct, followed: the map was the campaign's view. This is Maria's.
The straight-line problem was real: the pulse showed the machinery's perspective, which no voter ever experiences. The rework inverts it. The visitor watches an ordinary phone over an ordinary week; three campaign touchpoints arrive the way they actually arrive, unremarkable and human. Tap any of them to flip it over and see the chain that produced it, ending every time with the same green line: what stayed human. The page's argument: the machinery is invisible, and the disclosure question is whether that's honest.
