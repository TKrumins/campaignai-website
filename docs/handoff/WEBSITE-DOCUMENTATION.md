# 1. What CampaignAI Is

CampaignAI helps political and advocacy campaigns make **professional video, quickly and affordably**. The videos are produced with the help of AI and finished by human editors, so a campaign of any size can look and sound like it hired a top agency — without the agency price tag or the weeks of turnaround.

The website is our storefront and our argument. It explains the product, shows the pricing, tells the story of who's behind the company, lays out our ethics, and invites campaigns to book an onboarding call to get started.

**The core promise the site makes to a visitor:**

- Agency-quality campaign videos, starting at **$1,999** per video.
- A fast, guided process — plan at your pace, then receive a finished video within about 48 hours of submitting.
- The campaign stays in control of every creative decision; nothing ships without a human review.
- You own the final video outright — full rights, no watermark.

# 3. The Live Website, Page by Page

This is what a visitor sees today, and the key messaging on each page.

## The Home Page

The home page is the centerpiece and had the most work. It moves a visitor from "what is this" to "book a call," in this order:

1. **Opening (the hero).** A rotating showcase of our two demo films, with the headline *"Campaign videos for candidates"* (the last word rotates through candidates, advocacy groups, nonprofits, and more). It leads with the price — *"Agency-quality video, starting at $1,999 per video"* — and the two promotions.
2. **The Product.** Headline: *"Your campaign is bigger than one video. Tell the full story."* It makes the case that a campaign needs a series of videos across a race, shown as a timeline from launch to election day. It presents the video types we make — Announcement, Fundraising Appeal, Policy Explainer, and Get Out The Vote — plus three more marked *"Coming Fall 2026"*: Rapid Response, Contrast Ad, and Authentic & Candid.
3. **Pricing.** *"Professional video, priced for campaigns like yours."* Three options: the standard **$1,999** rate, the **$599** candidate rate for the 2026 cycle, and mission-based pricing for nonprofits ("Let's talk").
4. **Our films & reactions.** A showcase of our work and a strip of positive reactions.
5. **How It Works.** *"Guide your video from start to finish"* — you direct every decision, a human editor polishes every frame, and you download and deploy within 48 hours. A note reassures: *"Use as much or as little AI as you want."*
6. **Who We Serve.** *"Tactical videos. Built for your campaign."* — candidates, consultants, parties & PACs, nonprofits, and grassroots.
7. **Where your video goes.** *"Made to be shared, in real life"* — an interactive hub showing the many places a finished video travels: social, website, email, volunteers, events, donations, and even Connected TV and Broadcast TV.
8. **Who We Are.** *"Republican. Democrat. Forwardist."* — the founding team (Tom Krumins, Jermaine Johnson, Brandon Guffey) and strategic advisor Andrew Yang, deliberately spanning the political spectrum.
9. **Ethics.** *"We do the hard ethical work."* — our guardrails, and three promises we will never break (no impersonating a real person without written consent; never helping deceive voters about how/when/where to vote; never training outside AI models on a campaign's data).
10. **The cost problem.** Four figures that frame why this matters, closing on the line: *"Democracy has a paywall. Don't let the high costs stop your campaign."*
11. **FAQ**, then a **final call to action**: *"Campaigns move quickly. Start your next video today."*

## The Other Live Pages

- **Pricing** — the full pricing page: the three tiers, the America 250 offer, and answers to common questions.
- **Get Started** — the single place to begin: it books an onboarding call where we scope the video together, then invoice after. Nothing is charged upfront.
- **How Your Video Is Produced** — a step-by-step walkthrough of the whole process, from the first conversation through scripting, storyboarding, adding your own footage, voice and music, review, and final polish.
- **Where to Share Your Video** — a guide to every channel a finished video can run on, including Connected TV and a note about Broadcast TV (we deliver at broadcast quality but each station sets its own clearance rules).
- **Ethics** — the full ethics stance: our "truth in tech" philosophy, exactly where we draw the line, our policy on likenesses and deepfakes (consent is the rule; genuine satire is protected), and where compliance stands.
- **About** — the story of a company built "by people who are in the arena," the founding team, and the South Carolina origins.
- **Community** — how we build in the open: our newsletter, roundtables, and discussions.
- **AI in Campaigns** — an explainer and thought-leadership piece on where AI is taking campaigns.
- **Compliance** — a state-by-state look at disclosure rules (this page needs a careful review before we feature it — see Section 5).
- **Legal pages** — Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and related pages are live as placeholders pending final text.

# 4. Beyond the Live Site — Everything Else We've Built

A lot of work exists that isn't on the public site today. Some was built and deliberately parked; some was explored and set aside for a fuller pass later. None of it is lost.

## The five "For ___" audience pages *(built, then paused)*

We built dedicated landing pages for each audience — candidates, consultants, parties & PACs, nonprofits, and grassroots — each with its own tailored pitch and an interactive planner. We decided they **need a full redesign pass to be truly effective**, so we pulled them from the live site and set them aside as their own project. Today, anyone landing on one of those old addresses is smoothly sent to the Get Started page instead. The distinctive "democracy has a paywall" reveal graphic from that work was preserved to reuse when we rebuild them.

## The five interactive "Experience" pages *(built, held back)*

A set of interactive mini-experiences — *Through the Voter's Eyes, A Day on the Trail, The Campaign Machine, The Story Arc Builder,* and *The Disclosure Label Generator*. They're fully built and working, but hidden from the public site until we decide to feature them.

## Verified Human *(built, held back)*

A feature to prove a video was genuinely made and approved by a real campaign — a kind of authenticity badge and public verification. The finished page is complete and parked at a private address; the public version currently shows a "Coming Soon" placeholder.

## The Compliance depth *(live, but flagged)*

The compliance page and its interactive state-by-state tool exist and work, but the legal content needs a careful accuracy review before we lean on it in marketing.

## The Political Campaign Content Calendar *(a separate future project)*

A reference database of every date a campaign should track through an election cycle — primaries, deadlines, holidays, observances, and more. This is envisioned as its own separate website, not a page on this one. We have the plan and the seed data; no site has been built yet.

## Things we explored along the way

- **Several home-page opening designs** were built and compared before we chose the current rotating-film showcase.
- A **content calendar / "campaign arc" timeline**, a **growth explorer**, and numerous custom graphics and animations were designed to match the brand.
- A **newsletter pipeline** with branded cover art for upcoming articles.

# Technical Appendix

*For engineers and anyone who needs the operational detail. Not required reading for the sections above.*

## A. Technology & Hosting

- **Framework:** Next.js 16.2.2 (App Router), built as a fully **static export** — no server or database. Note: this is a customized Next build; consult the bundled docs in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing framework code.
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 with brand tokens as CSS variables. **Language:** TypeScript (strict).
- **Production hosting:** **GitHub Pages**, at campaignai.us (the domain is set by `public/CNAME`).
- **How a deploy happens:** work lives on the `feat/content-pages` branch; going live means merging it into `main` and pushing. A GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) builds and publishes to GitHub Pages on any push to `main`.
- **Preview:** a Vercel project ("quartz-lantern") auto-rebuilds a private preview whenever `feat/content-pages` is pushed. This is the review link.

## B. Critical operational facts (do not forget)

- **GitHub Pages ignores `vercel.json` entirely** — both its URL redirects and its "hide from search" header apply **only to the Vercel preview**, never to the live site.
- Because of that, production indexing is governed by `public/robots.txt` (allows crawling) plus per-page `noindex` tags on draft pages. Any redirect or search-visibility behavior for production must be built **in-app** (see `src/components/ui/RedirectStub.tsx`, used for retired `/purchase` and `/for` URLs).
- **Never push to `main` / deploy to production without explicit permission each time.** Preview-branch pushes are fine.
- The **repository is public** on GitHub — stage explicit file paths only (never a blanket add), and keep secrets out (`NEXT_PUBLIC_` values are effectively public by design).
- **Draft pages are hidden, not locked** — `noindex` + unlinked + out of the sitemap keeps them out of search, but anyone with the URL can view them.

## C. Brand system

- **Colors:** Regal Navy `#0D1B3E`, Liberty Crimson `#FF3366`, Freedom Blue `#4D9FFF`, Bridge Violet `#8E5CF7` (the "united" non-partisan accent), Beacon White `#E8F4F8`, Dawn Frost `#F5FAFC`, Verdant `#00D084` (Ethics only), Alert Amber `#FF9500` (roadmap/promotions tags), Pioneer Gold `#FFB800`.
- **Palette rules:** Multi-Partisan (red→violet→blue) signals "for every party"; real red-white-blue is the "Patriot" treatment; green is Ethics-only; pricing avoids a stark red-vs-blue split.
- **Signature motifs:** the four-point **AI Sparkle** (`src/components/ui/AISparkle.tsx`); the **favicon mark** beside each section eyebrow (`src/components/ui/SectionLabel.tsx`, assets in `public/assets/logos/`); the animated **Campaign Arc** timeline (`src/components/sections/home/CampaignArc.tsx`).
